Current Talk

Where your luck actually comes from

The standard advice is to post more and tell more people. New research from the Post-Industrial School found that founders spend the most time in the places least able to produce opportunity, and the least time in the places that produce the most. The missing variable is the environment: L = D × T × e.

This talk gives audiences three tests for any space their people spend time in, physical or digital, and a way to redesign where their hours go.

  • Why proximity, reach and volume don't produce the encounters that lead to opportunity
  • Three tests for any environment: is it bounded, is there a shared task and does it ask for a commitment?
  • A practical audit of where your hours go, and one change to make this month
